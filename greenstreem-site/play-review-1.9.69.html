GreenStreem foreground services demonstration

Play release 1.9.69. Demonstration uses an isolated Android TV emulator, a test Pro entitlement and an original synthetic color-bar stream. The app includes no TV content. Production purchase and promo-code entitlements remain managed by Google Play.

Data sync: scheduled recording

The user opens a guide program, chooses Schedule recording and a 30-second test. Recording starts on the scheduled alarm and continues after Home is pressed. Returning to Recordings shows Completed, and the saved file plays.

Connected device: recording-folder sharing

The user opens Shared recordings and explicitly enables sharing of the selected recording folder. A persistent foreground service serves files to authorized devices. The demo shows the active sharing screen and its Stop sharing control. A separate paired test client successfully listed and retrieved the completed 21,825,216-byte recording during this demonstration.

Recording and sharing use ongoing notifications. Users can stop recording or folder sharing. Casting additionally uses a connected-device foreground service while relaying media to a selected TV.
