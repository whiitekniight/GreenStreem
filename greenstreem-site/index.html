GreenStreem Recorder for Windows

Preview 0.1.0 · Record on your own PC while your phone, tablet or TV device is off. Save to an existing local, USB or NAS folder that Windows can access.

Download Windows Recorder ZIP
  1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder on your Windows PC. Open GreenStreem Recorder.exe from that folder.
  2. Choose your recording folder. For a NAS, open its shared folder in File Explorer and sign in first.
  3. In Android app 1.9.71 or newer, open Recordings → Set up PC recorder → Find my PC recorder.
  4. Click Connect a device on the PC and enter its code in the app.
  5. Schedule a guide program. Wait for PC confirmed the schedule before turning off your tablet or TV device.

Keep the PC on and signed in, the recorder running, and its storage connected. Minimize the recorder to keep it running. Preview supports one MPEG-TS recording at a time on your home network. Existing device recording remains available. Setup notes are included in the ZIP.
